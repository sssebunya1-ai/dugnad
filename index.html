About Dugnad School

Dugnad is a contemporary, fast-growing Kindergarten and Day Care in Kavule-Jomayi Estate, along Matuga–Semuto Road in Wakiso District, about an hour’s drive from Kampala.

The school offers formal and informal early childhood learning built on an activity-based curriculum, with a talented and experienced staff and a low teacher-pupil ratio. Children from diverse local and foreign backgrounds learn side by side.

Our philosophy: to voluntarily work together for a common goal (DUGNAD).

Motto

Academic programmes

Three levels, one approach: learner-centred and hands-on.

Daycare

Children from six months. All children rest after lunch and join selected educational activities from 3:30pm to 4:30pm.

Open 7:30am–4:30pm

Kindergarten

From three years. A test of numeracy and English literacy may be used on admission to decide the class.

Open 7:30am–12:30pm

Primary

Lower primary classes follow the same learner-centred method with ICT support.

Open 7:30am–4:30pm. Classes offered beyond P.3: to be confirmed by the school.

Clubs and sports

Scouts and Guides, IT, Chess, Music, Dance and Drama, Cookery, and Christian and Muslim networks. Club membership is 30,000 UGX per child and swimming lessons are offered as an option.

Admissions

What to bring

  • 2 passport-size photos of the child
  • 2 passport-size photos of the parents
  • A copy of the child’s birth certificate
  • The child’s immunisation card

Application forms are available from the Head Teacher’s office. Admission fee for new entrants: 50,000 UGX.

Parents can also request application forms through the contact details below.

School facilities

Computer learning lab

Tablets with headsets and about 200 educational apps covering logic, literacy and numeracy. Powered by Leap Learning Labs, Norway.

Security

Security personnel, a fenced compound and 24-hour CCTV. Children are released only to registered parents, guardians or care givers.

Feeding

Three balanced meals. Break tea, lunch and evening tea; lunch and evening tea are for daycare and primary.

Transport

A school van for pick-up and drop-off, with rates by distance.

Library and e-learning

A library and e-resources, with online classes for children who study from home at a cost.

Infrastructure

Adequate classrooms, water, reliable electricity, internet and toilet facilities.
